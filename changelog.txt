- Quick peek with particle effects, duck peek, autostop
- Zeusbot, knifebot, auto revolver, auto scope
- Player ESP: bounding box, skeleton, health bar, ammo bar, name, weapon, info flags
- Off-screen arrows with glow and directional indicators
- Player chams with 19 material types (liquid, metallic, glow, hologram, etc.)
- Backtrack chams and on-shot chams with fade
- Player glow for enemy, team, and local player
- Item ESP with text/icon display per weapon group
- Projectile ESP for all grenade types with damage indicators
- Bomb timer and spectator list with avatars
- Viewmodel chams for weapon, arms, and hands
- Bunny hop, air strafe, edge jump, edge bug, jump bug
- Fast ladder, edge stop, slow walk, no landing inaccuracy
- Advanced strafer with subtick yaw manipulation
- Custom FOV, thirdperson, freecam, custom aspect ratio
- Crosshair overlay, scope overlay replacement
- Velocity display with graph, keystrokes display
- Hit logs, miss logs, console logs, chat logs
- Hit sounds and death sounds (12 built-in + custom WAV)
- Hit effects and death effects with 12 particle types
- Bullet tracers, impact visualization, hit markers
- Grenade trajectory prediction with damage calculation
- Skin changer with paint kit, wear, seed, and StatTrak
- Knife changer, glove changer, agent changer
- Custom weapon models from disk
- Weather system: snow, rain, stars, ash, custom particles
- Custom fog, surface wetness, wind control
- Custom skybox, lighting, world color, bloom, gamma
- Smoke color, fire color, explosion color override
- Depth of field control, ambient lighting
- Remove smoke, flash reduction, remove visual recoil
- Remove decals, remove legs, remove scope, remove fog

- Clantag changer, name override, vote reveal

- Scoreboard weapon display via Panorama injection
- Widget system (modern, classic, neo, glass styles)
