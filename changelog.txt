## CHANGELOG

### 18.08.2026 – Update: Hats, Hitmarker & Extrapolation

**Hats (HUD > Hat)**
- 6 new hat types added: **Flower, Wizard, Cowboy, Heart, Bow, Party** → **14 hats in total** (Kasa, Bucket, Halo, Crown, Top Hat, Propeller, Horns, Star, Flower, Wizard, Cowboy, Heart, Bow, Party)
- **All hats animated**:
  - Propeller: spins
  - Star: slow rotation
  - Heart: tilt-wobble animation + glow
  - Top hat & others: additional animations
- Color selection (color + secondary color), glow + glow strength for all hats

**Hitmarker (Misc > Hit Marker)**
- **7 styles** in the selection menu: `cross`, `spin`, `plus`, `star`, `diamond`, `corners`, `circle`
- Every style with its own **rotation/animation**:
  - spin/star/diamond/corners/plus: rotate at different speeds
  - spin: additional size pulsing
- Expand animation on appear (as before), **glow for all styles**
- Types still: classic / damage / both

**Extrapolation completely rewritten (Rage > Aimbot > Extrapolation)**
- **3 selectable modes**: `linear`, `turn`, `adaptive`
  - linear: straight-line prediction
  - turn: accounts for rotations (turn rate)
  - adaptive: combines both based on target movement (recommended)
- **New settings** in popup:
  - `max ticks` (1–30, default 16)
  - `scale` (0–150 %, default 100 %)
  - `max turn rate` (1–60 °/tick, default 24)
- Weighted turn-rate tracking over up to 9 records, speed-trend detection, deadzone (0.75 °/tick), speed cap 320, abort when turn rate exceeded, coherence-based scaling in adaptive mode

**Misc**
- Improved hit/miss log analysis (miss reasons: lag compensation, spread, prediction error, occlusion, impact mismatch, server discrepancy, shoot position mismatch, death)
